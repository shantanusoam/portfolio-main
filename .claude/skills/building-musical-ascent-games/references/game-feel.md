# Game feel

Prioritize, in order:

- clear launch
- readable apex
- strong landing
- string/platform deformation
- squash and stretch
- responsive horizontal control
- forgiving early platforms
- short retry
- visible progress
- restrained screen effects

A technically-correct-but-floaty jump reads as broken even with perfect
collision math — tune anticipation/landing squash before tuning numeric
physics constants further.
