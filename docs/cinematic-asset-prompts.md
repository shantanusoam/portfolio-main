## Image prompts for matched future variants

### Meadow
Use the user’s screenshot as mood reference only. One original full-bleed cinematic scene, elevated camera, velvet emerald meadow filling the frame. No sky, horizon, mountains, trees, buildings, rocks or flowers. A tiny naturally spaced flock of white birds flies low across the central horizontal third. Diagonal lime-gold sunlight and deep green shade describe the smooth folds of land. Subtle dew glints, fine grass and soft optical glow. Dreamy, simplified CGI/cinema realism. Keep subjects in the center third for panoramic crop. No text, UI, frame, logos or collage.

### Shoreline
An elevated view looking diagonally down on a smooth emerald grassy bank and curved silver-turquoise water edge. No sky or mountains. A small anatomically natural white horse grazes at center-right around middle height. The landscape is spacious and softly luminous: fine grass, lime highlights, deep green shadows, exquisite restrained water glitter. Dreamlike calm with clean large forms. No people, buildings, paths, trees, rocks, phone UI, text or borders. Keep horse and water relationship within the middle third for a wide crop.

### Dusk
A tranquil lake at sunset, a small warm sun near center-right, smooth peach and rose sky fading into lilac, finely rippled lavender-blue water with pale pink-gold reflection. A small emerald grassy corner connects it to the other scenes. Delicate haze and optical bloom; spacious calm bands of colour. No dramatic mountains or clouds. Keep sun, horizon and reflection near the middle third. No people, boats, buildings, text, panels or UI.

## Image-to-video prompts — not generated yet

Use each corresponding approved still as the first frame. Generate separate shots, then edit the sequence; do not ask the generator to create the website UI or edit between shots.

### V1 / Meadow — 6 seconds
Locked elevated camera. Preserve every hill contour, bird identity, palette and exposure. Only a faint breeze moves the grass in a broad, slow wave. Keep the birds stationary in this first loop to avoid flock morphing and discontinuous entry/exit. No camera drift, texture crawl, new objects, audio or light flicker. Start and end on matching grass poses. If the seam fails, keep the still.

### V2 / Shore — 6 seconds
Locked camera and exact grass-bank geometry. Preserve the white horse’s anatomy, position and grazing pose. Animate only tiny water ripples and restrained specular glints, with a subtle breeze in the closest grass. Horse stays still; no leg changes or invented motion. No camera zoom, changing sunlight, new objects or audio. Seamless loop target, to be visually checked.

### V3 / Dusk — 6 seconds
Locked camera. Preserve the sun position, horizon, sky gradient and grassy bank. Only the fine water surface ripples and the existing sunset reflection responds gently. No time-lapse, changing cloud shapes, exposure pumping or new objects. Silent matching loop.

### Optional 12-second portfolio film
0–4s: meadow establishing shot. 4–8s: shore shot. 8–12s: sunset hold. Add the actual portfolio title, professional role and URL in an editor afterward. Use clean cuts and no generated typography.

## Delivery acceptance

Reject morphing birds, changing horse anatomy, hillside warping, glitter that flashes, changing exposure, and visible loop seams. Keep static posters and explicit Play/Pause. Do not attach video sources before opt-in. Pause when hidden/offscreen, and prefer stills under reduced motion. Aim for ≤1 MiB mobile / ≤2 MiB desktop per short loop; omit video if quality or loading cost is poor.
